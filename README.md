# Vektplan

Emil's personal app for eating a little more, a little more often — gently.

- **Today**: what you've eaten so far (calories, protein, fat, carbs) and when, one big "Log what you ate" button, ideas on the side if you want one, kind words, supplements with how many a day (3 creatine gummies, 1 multivitamin… tap once per one you take), stomach check-in
- **Food**: a diary you can step back through day by day, a 7-day chart with one coloured bar per day (protein, carbs, fat) and protein grams under it, and Vektplan's low-FODMAP recipes as ideas
- **Log**: one meal from several ingredients ("150 g beef + 50 g rice"): tap each food, type the amount in grams or everyday units, see calories and protein live, log it as one meal. Raw/dry vs cooked switch for meat, rice and pasta. Search, recently used, or browse by category. Type it ("150 g biff, 50 g ris") or snap a photo (AI estimate). Small energy boosters for when you get full fast
- **Train**: beginner muscle programs (Start here ×2, full body + focus day, full body ×3, upper/lower ×4) that adapt to where you train today (home or gym) and the equipment you have, with optional focus muscles, rep ranges, easier/harder versions, light days, and step-by-step how-to (what you need, steps, a tip, a video) for every exercise, plus an exercise guide. Or build your own plan from scratch. Log your own training outside the plan too ("arms for 25 min"): tick the muscles and exercises you did, how it felt, now or earlier. Plus a food-for-training guide
- **Progress**: trends for 7 days to a year (weight, eaten, burned from the Watch, training, supplements, day by day, CSV export), journal, a pattern finder that links food to symptoms, BMI
- **Coach**: quick help offline, full chat with an optional Claude API key
- **Apple Watch**: sleep, resting pulse, HRV, active calories, steps, exercise minutes and weight,
  brought in by a Shortcut (web apps can't read Apple Health directly). Let it run by itself each morning
  (Shortcuts automation), then tap Paste on Today. Guide in the app under Settings → Apple Watch.

It's a web app hosted on GitHub Pages. Open it in Safari on the iPhone and choose
**Share → Add to Home Screen**. It works offline, and all data stays on the phone.

## Updating the app

1. Change the code.
2. Run `python3 tools/bump.py 1.0.1 "What changed" "Hva som er nytt"`.
3. Commit and push to `main`.

The app checks for new versions when it opens. **Settings → Check for updates → Update now**
installs it straight away.

## Food data

Calories, protein, fat and carbs for the base foods come from Matvaretabellen 2026 (Mattilsynet),
Norway's official food composition table, published under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD).
To refresh them: clone `github.com/mattilsynet/matvaretabellen-deux` and run
`python3 tools/foodbase.py <clone>/data`. It writes `js/data/foodbase.js`.
